Arjun Ghosh

PhD Student, Department of Astronomy & Astrophysics, University of Chicago · McCormick Fellow

I build instruments to detect the faintest signals in the universe, from THz antennas for kinetic inductance detectors in dark matter searches to cryogenic radio receivers for cosmology. My work spans RF/mm-wave design, statistical design methods, and hands-on lab and telescope systems.

RF / mm-wave Design

MCMC-Based Design of Wideband Hybrid Couplers

2024 – present · Caltech / JPL · Advisor: R. Basu Thakur

A new, flexible method to design arbitrarily long symmetric quadrature hybrid couplers with a Markov Chain Monte Carlo solver. 3-, 5-, 7- and 9-section designs outperform reported designs; the same method also designs low-pass and band-pass filters.

Radio Cosmology

APSERa: Probing the Epoch of Recombination

2024 · Raman Research Institute · Advisors: M. S. Rao, K. Sathish

Built monitoring, control and data-acquisition web apps for a 2–4 GHz radio telescope. Achieved stable cryogenic runs at 75 K, 77 K and 120 K, tuned the cryocooler controller, designed a thermal shroud, and ran antenna/radome and RF-chain systematics simulations.

Low-Frequency Radio Astronomy

Passive-Mode Radio Astronomy with the CU-ST Radar

2022 – 2024 · Univ. of Calcutta & IIT Indore · Advisors: A. Datta, Patra, Paul

Turned a 53 MHz atmospheric radar into a passive radio telescope: RFI characterization, a Python analysis pipeline, and tracking of the Sun, Virgo A and Taurus A to calibrate the antenna beam.

Stellar Astrophysics

Magnetic Fields & Rotation of mCP Stars

2023 · Univ. of Moncton & Univ. of Delaware · Advisor: V. Khalack

Measured mean longitudinal magnetic fields of HD 99563 and HD 210684 from ESPaDOnS spectropolarimetry using least-squares deconvolution, refining the rotation period. Built a unified GUI pipeline around LSDpy and SpecpolFlow.
